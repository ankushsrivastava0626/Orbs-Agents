Orbs Agents

Talk to your coding agents.

Every coding-agent session you start gets an orb on your Mac's screen: Claude Code on Anthropic or OpenRouter models, or Codex. Hold the orb's number key, say what you want, and let go. The orb shows what the agent is doing, and Joe, an unflappable British butler, tells you when it's done.

Install Source on GitHub

Free and open source, for macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon.

Try it: hold 1 2 3 or 4 and let go.Try it: press and hold an orb, then let go.

How it works

Your agents keep running in their terminals. You stop reading them and start talking to them.

  1. 1

    Hold the orb's number key

    Orbs are numbered left to right, 1 to 0. The orb turns red and listens, and your speakers mute so nothing echoes.

  2. 2

    Talk, then let go

    Coral while your words are transcribed, then a green flash when they've been typed into that session.

  3. 3

    Look up when Joe speaks

    Purple while the agent works. Hover the orb to watch its steps. Blue while Joe tells you the outcome, or the question it needs you to answer.

More than talking

The same keys and orbs run everything else you'd otherwise type, tab or squint for.

Task cards

Hold `

A card fills with your words as you speak, and AI turns them into a prompt that's ready to send. Flip between your words and its version, and push the pointer into the top-left corner to see every card at once.

Meeting board

Double-tap right ⌥

Records your mic and your Mac's audio, transcribes it with speaker labels, and answers questions about the meeting so far. The board is hidden from screen sharing.

Notes

Hold left ⌥

Say it, and it's added to your notes file with the date and time. Click the card while it shows to fix a word first.

Read aloud

Double-tap right ⌘

Select text in any app and Joe reads it to you. Your clipboard is left exactly as it was.

Live steps

Hover a purple orb

See how long the agent has been at it, what it's doing right now and the steps before, updating as it goes.

Session search

Click + in the strip

Browse past conversations by folder and model, or search their words, and reopen any of them in an orb.

Auto mode

From an orb's menu

Hands-free: talk without holding anything. Joe stops the moment you hold a key to cut in.

OS mode

Double-tap left ⌥

A quiet full-screen workspace with its own menu bar, dock and app dial, your orbs always in reach.

Focus timer

From the menu

A small pill that counts down. When it ends, your screens go dark until you start the next one.

Where your voice goes

Orbs Agents is a few local programs and plain files. Here is exactly what leaves your Mac.

  • Nothing listens on your networkThe voice server answers on 127.0.0.1 only, and refuses requests from web pages and other hosts.
  • Zero data retention, every timeTranscription and Joe's voice go through OpenRouter, and every request asks for zero data retention and no data collection.
  • Or keep speech on your MacSwitch transcription to Parakeet from the orb menu and your words never leave the machine.
  • Silence isn't sentA local speech check drops recordings with no one talking before anything is uploaded.
  • Your key stays in your fileYour OpenRouter key is read from a file you choose when it's needed, and never copied or logged.
  • Your data is yoursNotes, task cards and meeting transcripts are ordinary files in ~/.claude/voice.

Install

About five minutes, most of it spent downloading Python packages.

You'll need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon on macOS 15 or later
  • Homebrew, then brew install tmux python@3.12
  • Claude Code
  • An OpenRouter key for transcription, Joe's voice and the boards
  • For Codex orbs, the Codex voice server as well

Then run

$ git clone https://github.com/ankushsrivastava0626/Orbs-Agents ~/.claude/voice
$ ~/.claude/voice/install.sh

Put your key in ~/.claude/voice/.env, allow VoicePet under System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility, click + at the bottom right of your screen, and hold 1.
