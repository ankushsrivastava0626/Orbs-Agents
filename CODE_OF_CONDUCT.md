# Code of conduct

Orbs Agents is for anyone who wants to talk to their tools. We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project, to feel welcome and safe, whatever their background,
identity or experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and take feedback the same way.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Never post someone's personal information, recordings or logs without consent.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or threats.
- Publishing others' private information.

## If something happens

Contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/ankushsrivastava0626). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove content, or warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in the
project's spaces or when they represent the project elsewhere.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
